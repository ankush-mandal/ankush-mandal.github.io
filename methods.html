Methods & Software

Numerical development

A dedicated home for algorithms, numerical modules, code development, validation tests, and computational infrastructure. This separates the question “what physics do I study?” from “what tools have I built?”

Self-gravity

Spectral–multigrid Poisson solver

A fast self-gravity solver for cylindrical and spherical geometries, combining Fourier decomposition in azimuth with geometric multigrid in the remaining two dimensions.

  • Finite-volume discretization in curvilinear coordinates
  • MPI-parallel multigrid
  • FFTW-based spectral decomposition
  • Integrated into PLUTO
Self-gravity

Cartesian Poisson solver

A three-dimensional multigrid Poisson solver for Cartesian meshes, including Runge-Kutta-Legendre acceleration for relaxation-based gravity calculations.

Star formation

Sink particles

A sink-particle module for modelling gravitationally bound collapsing objects, designed for self-gravitating astrophysical simulations.

Subgrid physics

Turbulence-regulated star-cluster model

A subgrid framework for estimating star formation using local turbulence and thermodynamics rather than a simple density threshold.

Analysis

Parallel FellWalker / ClumpFind

A parallel structure-identification algorithm for finding and characterizing dense structures in multidimensional simulation data.

PLUTO development

Extensive development and extension of PLUTO, including hydrodynamic/MHD algorithms, reconstruction, Riemann solvers, time integration, parallel decomposition, and new physics modules.
