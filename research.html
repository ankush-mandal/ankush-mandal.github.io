Research topics

Each topic can combine a short scientific overview, a simulation movie, selected results, and links to the relevant publications. The movies below are simple HTML5 video elements and work directly on GitHub Pages.

Current

Gravitational instability & fragmentation

I use global three-dimensional simulations to study the nonlinear evolution of massive protoplanetary disks, including spiral structure, thermal self-regulation, fragmentation, and the survival of gravitationally bound structures.

Thermodynamics

Shock heating in fragmenting disks

I am investigating how shocks set the thermal balance of gravitationally unstable disks, how irreversible shock heating can be measured robustly, and how numerical shock treatment affects fragmentation thresholds.

Future direction

Magnetic fields & dust

My disk work is expanding toward the interaction of self-gravity with magnetic fields and dust dynamics, including magnetic amplification, angular-momentum transport, and dust concentration in GI structures.

Earlier work

AGN feedback & star formation

My doctoral research studied how relativistic jets and AGN-driven outflows interact with multiphase gas, molecular clouds, turbulence, and star formation, combining galaxy-scale simulations with subgrid star-formation modelling.
